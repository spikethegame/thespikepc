The Spike Steam Version: What You Need to Know

The Spike first launched on Steam in June 2020. This was a standalone PC version separate from mobile. It offered local multiplayer and Steam achievements.

In 2023, developers announced the original Steam version would be discontinued. The mobile and Steam versions had separate development paths. This created update and operation problems.
